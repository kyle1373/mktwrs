Yeah that's the case since Reply #26 with the 1'04"96 by とめごめ but we now have かぐやん's explanation, and in English:

"The reason for changing the setting of “Handle Assist” is mainly because of “Ease of accumulating turbo during drift” and “Invasion of bad road”.

In order to collect the turbo quickly on the first curve, set "Handle Assist" to "On" so that you can not enter the rough road when drifting.

Also, since the shortcut just before the goal is based on the premise of entering a rough road, "handle assist" is set to "off" in anticipation of vacant timing in the course."
